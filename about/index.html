About

An evolving home for sports economics.

Sportsecon is being built as a flexible hub for sports economics tools, writing, research, and related projects. The current beta is deliberately simple: it establishes a coherent foundation that can expand over time.

Purpose

Bridge research and usable analysis.

The site is meant to sit between academic work and practical sports analysis. Some pages will be explanatory, some will be interactive, and some will simply house ongoing work.

Growth path

Built for future additions.

Over time the site may include more tools, a larger writing archive, and personal or collaborator pages under their own routes. The current structure keeps that growth straightforward.
